PAYMENT POLICY

VoiceMind E-Commerce supports online payments through available payment methods.

Payment Failure:
If a payment fails:
1. Ask the customer to verify their payment details.
2. Ask them to retry the payment.
3. If the problem continues, support should investigate the issue.

Important:
The support agent must never request or expose sensitive payment information such as passwords, PINs, CVVs, or full card numbers.

If a payment issue cannot be resolved using available information, the customer should be offered human support.
