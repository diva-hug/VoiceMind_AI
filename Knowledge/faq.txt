VOICE-MIND E-COMMERCE FAQ

Q: How can I check my order status?
A: Customers can check their order status using their order ID. If the order information is unavailable, the customer should contact customer support.

Q: How long does delivery usually take?
A: Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order has been shipped.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving an eligible product.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: After a return is approved and processed, refunds usually take 5 to 7 business days to appear in the customer's original payment method.

Q: What should I do if my payment failed?
A: Customers should first verify their payment details and try the payment again. If the payment continues to fail, customer support should investigate the issue.

Q: What if my order is delayed?
A: Customers should provide their order ID so that the support system can check the current order and delivery information.

Q: Can I speak to a human support agent?
A: Yes. Customers can request a human support agent at any time.
