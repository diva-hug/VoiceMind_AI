CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers can request cancellation before an order has been shipped.

Before Shipment:
The cancellation request may be processed if the order has not yet been shipped.

After Shipment:
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible. The customer may need to follow the applicable return process instead.

Important:
The support agent must check the available order information before confirming cancellation.

The support agent must never claim that an order was cancelled unless the available system information confirms it.
