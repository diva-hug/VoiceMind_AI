DELIVERY POLICY

Standard Delivery:
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after an order has been shipped.

Delivery Delays:
Delivery may take longer because of:
- Weather conditions
- Carrier delays
- High order volume
- Incorrect delivery information
- Operational issues

Delayed Orders:
If a customer reports a delayed order, the support agent should request the order ID and check the available order information.

Important:
The support agent must never invent a delivery date or order status.
If current order information is unavailable, the customer should be informed honestly and offered human support when necessary.
