REFUND POLICY

VoiceMind E-Commerce provides refunds for eligible returned products.

Refund Eligibility:
- The customer must request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.
- The product must be eligible for return.
- The product should be returned according to the company's return instructions.
- The return must be approved before the refund is processed.

Refund Process:
1. Customer requests a return.
2. Support verifies the order and return eligibility.
3. The return is approved if the product meets the requirements.
4. Customer returns the product.
5. The returned product is processed.
6. Refund is initiated to the original payment method.

Refund Timeline:
Refunds usually take 5 to 7 business days after the return has been processed.

Important:
The support agent must never promise a refund unless the available information confirms that the customer is eligible.
